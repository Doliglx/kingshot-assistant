## Feedback from beta.3

### Rally Timer
- **Turrets.** Pick *Castle* or *Turret*, then North, South, East or West. The copied message names the turret in the chosen language ("WEST TURRET HIT", "ATTAQUE DE LA TOURELLE OUEST"…).
- **Exact UTC: launching from the game.** The floating bubble and its notification are gone from this tab. Instead, a guide with an annotated screenshot of the game explains how a leader launches on the exact second: prepare the rally, stay on the rally screen, read the game's UTC clock, and press Deploy at the launch time minus the rally time. The guide can be hidden in one tap and brought back. Quick Sync keeps its bubble.

### Auto Clicker
- **Reorganised in four tabs**: Macros, Edit, Look, Share. Targets are compact lines that open one at a time, so the page no longer grows with every target.
- **Durations in ms, seconds or minutes**, decimals allowed (e.g. 1.5 min). The floating panel shows values in the most readable unit too.
- **Marker size to the dp**, from 16 to 96, with a live preview.
- **👁 on the panel** hides every marker in one tap (touches then go through to the game).
- **Folded panel** keeps only ▶, ⚙ and ✕. Any panel button can be used to drag it.
- **The panel follows the app's accent colour**, like the Rally Timer bubble.
- **Ready-made macros**: *Batch healing* is now one target tapped every 20 ms; new *Terror* macro launches a Terror rally every 2 minutes. Both are added the first time the module opens.

### Look
- Red neon app icon and logo.
- New footer: *Made with ❤️ by Doliprane · Kingdom 1167*, in neon in your accent colour.
