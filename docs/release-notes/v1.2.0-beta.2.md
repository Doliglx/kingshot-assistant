## Auto Clicker: faster, and fully controllable from the floating panel

### Fixed
- **The interval you typed was sometimes ignored.** A value was only saved when you left its field, so switching straight to the game kept the default 1000 ms. Values are now saved as you type.

### Changed
- **Much lower limits.** The interval can go down to 1 ms and a tap now lasts 20 ms by default (it was fixed at 40 ms). The tap duration is a setting of its own.
- Android itself needs a few tens of milliseconds per tap, so that is the real floor; the panel no longer adds anything on top of it.
- The run counter refreshes a few times per second instead of on every tap.
- A macro no longer stops after five failed taps in a row; it stops when no tap has gone through for two seconds, which is what happens when you keep a finger on the screen.

### New: everything from the floating panel
Press ⚙ on the panel to open its settings, without going back to the app:
- switch between saved macros, or create a new one;
- interval, start delay, and when to stop (manual, after N cycles, after N seconds);
- per target: action (tap, long press, swipe), its duration, the wait after it and its repeat count;
- ＋ adds a target (a single-target macro becomes a sequence), 🗑 removes the selected one;
- tap a marker to select its target; the selected marker gets a yellow ring.

Values use − / + buttons (hold to change faster): a floating panel cannot open the keyboard.
