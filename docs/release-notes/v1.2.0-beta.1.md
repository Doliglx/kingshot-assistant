## New beta — two features in test

**Rally Timer** (beta) and **Auto Clicker** (test) join the app, which also gets a new home screen.

### New
- **Rally Timer** — land every rally on the same second.
  - *Exact UTC*: enter the arrival time, get each leader's launch time. The clock is synchronised with an external source and shows its accuracy (± ms).
  - *Quick Sync*: no clock needed; the slowest leader launches first, the others follow at an offset.
  - Copy-ready message for your alliance chat, in 8 languages.
  - Android: a floating countdown bubble over the game, with sound and vibration at T−3, T−2, T−1, GO.
- **Auto Clicker** — a floating panel that taps for you.
  - Single target in a loop, or a sequence of targets saved as a macro (tap, long press, swipe, per-step delay and repeat).
  - Stop after a number of cycles, after a duration, or by hand. Batch-healing template included.
  - Runs through a gestures-only accessibility service: it cannot read the screen.
- **New home screen** — module tiles that open with an animation, a settings sheet with accent colour and language.
- **8 languages**: English (default), French, German, Spanish, Portuguese (BR), Italian, Turkish, Russian.

### Changed
- March Ratio now always adds up to the total; Gather Time keeps its finish time current; inputs reject negative numbers.
- Frakinator searches every 1 % instead of every 2 %.

### Removed
- Gift Codes (deprecated) and Event Exchanger.

### Good to know
- **Signing key changed.** Uninstall any earlier beta before installing this one.
- The floating bubble and the auto clicker are new: please report anything odd.
- Most games forbid automation in their terms of service. Using the auto clicker in a game is at your own risk.
