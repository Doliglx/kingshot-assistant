## Auto Clicker, third round

Builds on 1.2.0-beta.2 (faster taps, everything adjustable from the floating panel) and adds:

### New
- **Simultaneous mode.** Every target is pressed at the same moment, like several fingers (up to the number of fingers your phone supports). Waits and repeats do not apply; the interval sets the pace between presses.
- **Live counters.** While a macro runs the panel shows the number of taps (or cycles done / goal) and the elapsed time.
- **Fold the panel.** ◂ folds it down to a small floating button with play/stop and the counter; ▸ unfolds it.
- **Appearance**, from the app or the panel (⚙): marker size (small, medium, large), panel opacity, and an option to hide the markers completely while a macro runs.
- **Share macros.** Copy a macro as text and paste it in alliance chat; on another phone, paste it under *Share → Import*. Imported macros never replace existing ones. Positions are screen pixels, so on a phone with a different screen the markers need to be moved.
- **Mode switch on the panel** between "one after another" and "all at once" once a macro has two targets or more.

### Not included, on purpose
- **Random intervals and positions** ("anti-detection"). Their only purpose is to hide automation from a game, which is exactly what most games' terms forbid.
